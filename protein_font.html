Under the hood

How Was This Made?

ESM-Fold2 is a protein-structure prediction model: give it an amino-acid sequence, and it predicts the three-dimensional structure that sequence may adopt. For this alphabet, we use that process in reverse.

01

Start with a target shape

Each letter is converted into a target three-dimensional backbone trace—the structure we want a protein to resemble.

02

Optimize the sequence backward

At every position, the pipeline keeps soft scores, or logits, for all 20 amino acids. It folds those scores, measures the difference from the target, and backpropagates the error to improve the sequence.

03

Re-fold, rotate, and select

We repeat the optimization many times, fold the resulting discrete sequences again from scratch, and keep the candidate whose rendered silhouette looks most like the intended letter.

These are computational design hypotheses, not experimentally validated proteins. They may fold as predicted, but testing would be required to know how they behave in the lab. The full pipeline is available in the shape2prot repository.
